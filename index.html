Before you start

  • This test has 20 questions, randomly picked from a larger question bank — so it's a little different every time.
  • Questions are true/false or multiple choice. Some include a screenshot from Tinkercad.
  • Once you pick an answer for a question and move on, you can't go back — so take your time.
  • You'll see your score and whether you passed at the end. A score of 80% (16 out of 20) or higher is a pass.
  • You can retake the test as many times as you'd like to practice.
  • Your name goes on your results so your teacher can tell it's really you.
